Collection Design

What This Site Will House

Released PDFs

Direct links to declassified scans hosted by the Bush Presidential Library and National Archives for meetings likely to matter to the Western Europe volume.

Document Metadata

Date, participant, country, title, release status, NAID, source collection, FRUS volume tag, chapter assignment, and whether the item is full release, partial release, or still withheld.

Research Notes

Short, separable notes for German unification, NATO, the European Community, summit diplomacy, and country files without altering the archival citation.

Declassification Trail

A place to track FOIA or mandatory review identifiers, partial releases, denied records, and later openings while the official FRUS volume is still researched.
