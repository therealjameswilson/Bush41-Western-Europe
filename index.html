FRUS 1989-1992, Volume VIII

Western Europe Memcons and Telcons

A companion archive for declassified memoranda and telephone conversations relevant to the forthcoming Foreign Relations of the United States, 1989-1992, Volume VIII, Western Europe, arranged in four chapters: United Kingdom, France, Italy, and Regional, with a separate Germany reference section for Kohl, East German, and related German-leader conversations, plus a Public Papers reference section for President Bush's Western Europe statements. Records inside each section are ordered chronologically.

What This Site Will House

Released PDFs

Date, participant, country, title, release status, NAID, source collection, FRUS volume tag, chapter assignment, and whether the item is full release, partial release, or still withheld.

Research Notes

Short, separable notes for German unification, NATO, the European Community, summit diplomacy, and a Germany reference section without altering the archival citation.
