FRUS 1989-1992, Volume VIII

Western Europe Memcons and Telcons

A companion archive for declassified memoranda and telephone conversations relevant to the forthcoming Foreign Relations of the United States, 1989-1992, Volume VIII, Western Europe, arranged in five chapters: United Kingdom, France, Italy, Regional, and the United States and the CSCE, 1989-1992. A separate Germany reference section gathers Kohl, East German, and related German-leader conversations, alongside a Public Papers reference section. Records inside each section are ordered chronologically and linked back to the scan.
