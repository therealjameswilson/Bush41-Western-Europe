Analytic Threads

Core Themes

German Unification

How Washington worked with Bonn, London, Paris, and Moscow to manage the diplomatic architecture of a unified Germany inside NATO.

NATO After the Cold War

The alliance moved from Cold War deterrence toward a post-1989 security order while preserving transatlantic confidence.

European Community

U.S. policy tracked European integration, Maastricht negotiations, trade disputes, and the changing balance between national capitals and Brussels.

Balkan Crisis

The breakup of Yugoslavia tested U.S.-European coordination and exposed sharp questions about recognition, intervention, and burden sharing.
